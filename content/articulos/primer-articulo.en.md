Minimalism is not just about having fewer material things, but about making room for what truly matters. In this first article, we will explore the roots of this philosophy and how to apply it in our daily lives.

## The First Step

To begin, we must observe our surroundings and ask ourselves what objects bring real value. Anything that does not serve a purpose or bring us joy is likely occupying space that could be used for mental peace.

The Japandi philosophy combines Japanese minimalism with Scandinavian design. This union teaches us that a serene and functional environment can enhance our creativity and well-being.
