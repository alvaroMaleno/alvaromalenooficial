We live in an age where constant noise fills every corner of our day. Silence is not a void to be filled, but a canvas upon which our true thoughts can be drawn. By embracing empty space, we are losing nothing, but gaining the whole world.

We look around and everything becomes "I". The ego invades our world, standing between reality and ourselves. In its fantastic illusion, sound becomes that which disturbs us, the people around us become the threat that keeps us on alert, and even an inert object, when it appears imposing and magical on our screens, becomes the object we desire to possess, the source of our virtues.

Without ego, a car is just a moving vehicle, an object with wheels capable of transporting us from one place to another. What other people say becomes a mere vibration in the air, devoid of meaning unless we endow it with one, and an object on a screen is just a set of pixels showing us an image.

Without ego, silence is our life, our calm, our freedom. The Present.
